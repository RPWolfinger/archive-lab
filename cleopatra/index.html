Think Like a Historian

  • Cleopatra was Greek by family, ruling Egypt, portrayed in Roman marble. Whose image of her is this?
  • Why would a queen of Egypt wear the headband of Greek kings rather than an Egyptian crown?
  • Most stories about Cleopatra were written by Romans after her death. How might that change the story?
  • Why might so many ancient statues have broken noses?

How to use the Archive Lab

  • Drag the portrait to turn it. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Click a label, or click the portrait itself, to learn about that part.
  • Desk buttons: spin, zoom, labels on/off, real size, and this help.
  • This is a 3D scan of a plaster cast, so it carries shape only. The painted colours are a reconstruction: ancient marble portraits were painted, and the Berlin portrait of Cleopatra still has faint red paint in its hair. The skin and diadem colours are educated guesses. Use "Show bare marble" to see the stone. The stand is modern.
